What's Driving You?

A Podcast About More Than Being a Dad

Dad Drive started with a simple idea: dads are carrying a lot, and most of us are figuring it out as we go.

We're husbands, fathers, employees, leaders, coaches, problem-solvers, drivers, and the people everybody seems to call when something breaks.

The Dad Drive podcast creates a place to talk honestly about all of it—the wins, mistakes, pressure, humor, uncertainty, and lessons we're learning along the way.

The goal isn't perfection. It's becoming a little more intentional about how we show up for our families, our work, our communities, and ourselves.

You don't need to have fatherhood figured out.

None of us do. Keep showing up. Keep learning. Keep moving forward. One drive at a time.

The Six Lanes

What We Talk About

Fatherhood touches almost every part of life. Dad Drive follows six lanes that help frame the conversations.

Fatherhood

Showing up for the people who call you Dad.

Marriage

Building a relationship while building a life together.

Leadership

Leading at home, at work, and when nobody gives you the playbook.

Work

Career, responsibility, ambition, burnout, and figuring out what's next.

Purpose

Who are you beyond your job, paycheck, and responsibilities?

Life

Kids. Money. Aging. Sports. Technology. Mistakes. Humor. All of it.
