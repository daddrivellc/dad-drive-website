Real Life From the Front Seat

The School Run Became a Show

Some of the best conversations between parents and kids happen when nobody planned to have a conversation at all. Sometimes they happen over dinner. Sometimes they happen late at night. Around here, a lot of them happen in the car.

Daddy Daughter Dropoff captures those conversations between a dad and daughter during the everyday drive to school. There's no boardroom, no studio full of producers, and usually no idea where the conversation is going next.

One morning might involve coffee shops and school traffic. Another might turn into opinions about music, movies, trends, family life, growing up, or why Dad apparently knows nothing about what teenagers think is cool.

That's the point. It's a real snapshot of a father-daughter relationship while life is actually happening.

Childhood happens in ordinary moments.

Sometimes the five-minute conversation on the way to school becomes the part you remember years later.
